How to read this map

Every story has one owning epic. Related-epic links do not increase the epic counts. Direct product includes user and user-developer behavior. Developer stories own reusable mechanisms and contracts in any project; an adapter location counts as direct product evidence when it holds a product rule.

All {{count code_files}} C# files were read. The other {{count aux_files}} inputs are project files, dependency locks, configuration, grammars, licenses, and icons. Tests, scripts, CI, and generated build outputs are outside the map. Source locations are methods, types, or file-level evidence.

A review candidate has at least five direct product stories or at least three owning product epics. This is a navigation aid. The catalogue records implemented behavior from source.

Feedback IDs, injected services, and serialized fields are dependencies of the stories they serve.
